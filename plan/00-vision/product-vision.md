# Product vision

## What this is
A B2B sales CRM: contacts, companies, deals moving through a pipeline, and the activity history that explains why they moved.

## Who uses it
- **Sales rep** — lives in it all day. Wants to log a call and advance a deal in seconds, and never re-type anything a system already knows.
- **Sales manager** — wants an accurate pipeline and forecast, and to see which reps and deals are stalling.
- **Admin / RevOps** — configures pipelines, custom fields, roles, and integrations.

## The problems we're solving
1. **Leads arrive everywhere and get lost.** Facebook lead forms, inbound email, web forms — they land in inboxes and spreadsheets, not in one queue with an owner and an SLA.
2. **Manual data entry eats selling time.** Reps re-type what's already in an email or a lead form.
3. **The pipeline isn't trusted.** Stages are stale because updating them is a chore, so the forecast is fiction.
4. **No single history per customer.** Emails, calls, and notes live in three places.

## What "solved" looks like
- A lead from any connected source is in the CRM, deduped, owner-assigned, **and its owner notified** within one minute of submission — with zero manual entry.
- A rep's daily loop (log activity, advance deal, add contact) is under 10 seconds each.
- The manager's forecast is derived from data reps update as a byproduct of working, not as extra work.

## Not this product
Marketing automation suite, customer support ticketing, ERP/invoicing, or a general no-code app builder.
